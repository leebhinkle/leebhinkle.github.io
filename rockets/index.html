Welcome to Lee's Rocket Page

My attempt to describe and organize some of my rocket experiments while also playing with generative AI for website work!

The Egg Rocket is a finless spin-stabilized rocket. The rotation keeps it mostly pointing up due to the gyroscopic effect like a toy top or rifle bullet. The Egg is a pretty simple 3D printed design, the launch pad to spin it is a bit more complicated. Here is a video describing the design including a failed and finally successful launch.

The Gingerbread Rocket is made from gingerbread except for the internal body tube, motor mount, and recovery components. Here is a short launch video and a longer build video.
